## 🏮 The Vision
**JLPT Samurai** isn't just a flashcard app; it's a cognitive training ground. By combining the discipline of a Samurai Dojo with modern **Spaced Repetition Systems (SRS)** and gamified engagement, this platform ensures that every minute spent studying translates into long-term neurological retention. 

Whether you are conquering Hiragana for the first time or grinding through N4 Kanji, JLPT Samurai provides the tools to slash through the complexity of the Japanese language.

---

## 🏟️ Core Learning Modules

### ⚡ Kana Dojo
A comprehensive training grid for **Hiragana** and **Katakana**. 
- **Why?** Foundations must be unbreakable. 
- **Features:** Native audio feedback, Romaji toggles, and dedicated speed-drills to ensure recognition becomes a reflex.

### 📜 Vocabulary Dojo (N5 & N4)
Access thousands of curated words organized by JLPT level.
- **Why?** Vocabulary is the ammo of communication.
- **Features:** Real-time search, native speech synthesis, and "Reveal Romaji" mechanics to prevent reliance on phonetic aids.

### 🀄 Kanji Dojo
Master the strokes and meanings of essential Japanese characters.
- **Features:** Comprehensive detail panels including Onyomi/Kunyomi readings, meanings, and contextual examples.

---

## 🛠️ Advanced Learning Systems

### 🧠 Smart Flashcard Engine (Anki-Style)
Our 3D Flashcard system is engineered for deep focus.
- **Persistent Sessions:** The engine automatically saves your exact progress. If you exit on Card #42, you start on Card #42 next time.
- **No Arbitrary Limits:** Study at your own pace. The deck adapts to you, not a fixed timer.
- **Metacognition:** Self-rate your recall (Again, Hard, Good, Easy) to feed the SRS algorithm.

### ⚔️ Samurai Quiz Engine
High-stakes testing to validate your progress.
- **Customizable Length:** Choose between a quick **20-word skirmish** or a **Full Lesson Siege**. 
- **Smart Selection:** The engine prioritizes your weakest words without repeating them needlessly, ensuring maximum efficiency.
- **New Rewards:** Earn XP for every correct answer, with massive bonuses for high-accuracy streaks.

### ❤️‍🩹 The Infirmary
The place where wounded warriors go to recover. 
- **Weakness Tracking:** Any word you miss in a quiz is automatically sent to the Infirmary.
- **Mandatory Recovery:** To clear the Infirmary, you must study the flashcards and pass a **Full-Length Quiz** on every single weak word. No shortcuts allowed.

## 🏆 Gamification & Progression

- **XP & Ranking System:** Progress through the ranks from **Ashigaru** to **Shogun**. Every word mastered and quiz conquered adds to your global glory.
- **Daily Streaks:** Consistency is the soul of language learning. Maintain your streak to earn massive XP multipliers.
- **Global Leaderboard:** Compete in real-time with other Samurai across the globe via Firebase-backed global syncing.

## ⚙️ Technical Architecture

- **Engine:** Pure ES6+ JavaScript. No heavy frameworks—just high-performance, optimized code for instant load times.
- **Database:** **Google Firebase & Firestore** for real-time state synchronization and authentication.
- **Design System:** Custom-built "Bushido Dark" CSS theme, fully responsive for Desktop, Tablet, and Mobile.
- **Audio:** Integrated Web Speech API for low-latency, native Japanese pronunciation.
